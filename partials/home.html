Machine Learning Inference

Re-thinking how operational insight is delivered.

Inference systems that extract flow, state, and condition from operational data — deployable, scalable, and trusted.

The problem

It’s time to rethink how insight is generated in physical systems.

Many of the quantities that matter most in industrial assets — such as flow, internal operating state, and equipment condition — are expensive or impractical to measure directly. Phluux leverages inference to close this gap, using machine-learning inferencers to replace or augment traditional system outputs without requiring additional instrumentation. The result is lower capital and operating cost, combined with operational insight that can be validated, deployed, and trusted over time.

What Phluux delivers

Inference systems that estimate operational quantities from existing data: flow rates, equipment wear, system behavior. Not dashboards. Not alerts. Actual estimates with quantified uncertainty, validated across the operating envelope, and packaged for runtime deployment.

Flow inference

Estimate flowrates without adding instrumentation. Enable allocation, optimization, and performance monitoring at scale.

State inference

Infer operating mode, phase, or regime from sensor patterns. Support control logic and condition-dependent models.

Condition inference

Detect wear, degradation, or anomalies before they cause failures. Plan interventions with lead time.

From models to learning systems

The shift from building individual models to building learning systems: a structured process that defines coverage, validates across the operating envelope, packages for deployment, and monitors in production.

This is what enables inference systems to scale from one asset to many, to adapt to changing conditions, and to maintain trust over time.

01

Coverage-driven scenarios

Synthetic data generation ensures the model sees the full operating envelope — including rare conditions that never appear in historical logs.

02

Validation upfront

Acceptance criteria defined before training. Performance validated across the envelope, not just on a holdout set from normal operations.

03

Runtime packaging

Models automatically packaged with schemas, preprocessing, versioning, and validity envelopes. A single function handles the complete packaging workflow, ready for integration, not research artifacts.
