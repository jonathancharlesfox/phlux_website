Mission

Phluux was founded to bring production-grade inference into real operational workflows for physical assets. We focus on making machine learning deployable, scalable, and trusted — not just accurate in a lab notebook.

Background

Jonathan Fox brings deep experience in flow assurance and industrial process systems, combined with a strong foundation in physics-based simulation, machine learning, and project delivery. After years of delivering inference solutions through managed engagements, the repeatable learning system — coverage-driven scenarios, acceptance criteria upfront, full-envelope validation, and deployment-ready packaging — became Phluux Studio.

How we work

Whether you use Phluux Studio or engage us for managed delivery, you get the same learning system and deployment approach. The difference is who runs the workflow.
