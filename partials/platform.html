Key capabilities

Phluux Studio provides the capabilities needed to build trusted inference systems. Every capability is designed to support validation, transparency, and deployment readiness in physical systems.

Coverage Audit

Systematically assess scenario coverage across the full operating envelope, including stress interactions, degraded conditions, and edge cases. Identify coverage gaps before training begins — not after deployment.

Knowledge Generation & Scenario Design

Generate and manage large sets of synthetic scenarios directly through the interface. Explore operating envelopes, condition sampling, and uncertainty between physics-based models and field behavior in a structured, repeatable way.

Machine Learning Canvas

Configure and train models with full control over architecture, features, and hyperparameters. Expert mode enables manual design, while guided workflows support structured exploration without hiding assumptions.

Model Passport

Versioned inference artifacts that include schema definitions, preprocessing pipelines, validation results, and deployment envelopes. Every model is traceable, reproducible, and auditable.

Deployment & Runtime Packaging

Package inference models as containerized runtimes ready for cloud or on-prem deployment. Each runtime includes versioned schemas, preprocessing, and monitoring hooks for reliable integration.

Phluux Studio uses physics-based system representations to generate structured training data for learning and inference workflows. The platform is designed to expand its supported system models and components over time, with a roadmap focused on increasing physical fidelity and coverage.

Avoiding the black box

Phlux Studio uses transparent, data generation and ML workflows where every step—from data preparation to model training and validation—is explicit and traceable. Inputs, preprocessing logic, feature engineering, and model assumptions are documented alongside validation results and operating envelope, ensuring models are explainable, auditable, and deployable with confidence.
